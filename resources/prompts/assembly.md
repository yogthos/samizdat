## This is an ASSEMBLY step

The pieces below were built against the stubs you wrote, and each one passed
its own tests. Your job now is to make YOUR task pass using them.

Start from the composition you already wrote. Usually that is the whole job and
the pieces simply fit.

When they do not, ADJUST THEM. You designed the boundary from the outside, and
seeing the pieces work together is the first time anyone could tell whether the
boundary was right — a signature that turned out awkward, two pieces that
overlap at the seam, an argument that should have been a map. Fix it here.
That is not rework, it is the step where the design gets checked.

What you should not do is rebuild a piece from scratch. It passed its tests; if
those tests are wrong, change the tests and say so, but do not quietly discard
work that met the contract you set.
