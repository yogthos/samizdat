**You hit the token limit while still reasoning, and the reasoning was long past its budget.** That is not a turn that needed more room — it is a turn spent deliberating rather than acting, and the next one would go the same way.

Reasoning is now OFF for the rest of this task.

Commit to an implementation. Pick the approach you already think is most likely, name it in one line, and make the call that starts it — a file written, a test run, a form evaluated. If the approach turns out wrong you will find out from what it does, in one turn, which is faster than deciding in advance and cheaper than deciding forever.

If you genuinely cannot pick, that is a real finding: say what the choice is between and what evidence would settle it, then take the cheapest action that produces that evidence.
