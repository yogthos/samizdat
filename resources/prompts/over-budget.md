This task is larger than one task.

That is a measurement, not an opinion: the lines below are what you have
actually written since you claimed it, added and deleted, counted from the
tree rather than estimated.

A task this size is the shape that lands nothing. It spends its whole budget
on a half-built whole, and the review at the end has to judge all of it at
once — so a defect anywhere sends the entire thing back, including the parts
that were finished and correct.

Do one of two things, now, before writing more:

  - **Split it.** `task create` a subtask per part with `parentId` set to this
    task, then `task switch` to the first. Each part then gets its own review,
    and the parts that land stay landed. What you have already written is not
    lost — it belongs to whichever part it implements.

  - **Say why it is one change.** If this genuinely cannot be divided — one
    coherent edit that happens to be large, a mechanical rename across many
    files — then say so in a sentence and carry on. That is a legitimate
    answer and this will not ask again for a while.

What is not an option is continuing without deciding. The budget is a prompt
to make the call while there is still budget left to act on it.
